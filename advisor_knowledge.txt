Institution: Cascade State University — School of Computer & Data Sciences

DEGREE PROGRAMS
- BS in Computer Science (120 credits): Core sequence is CS101 Intro to Programming -> CS201
  Data Structures -> CS301 Algorithms -> CS310 Systems -> CS401 Capstone. Requires one AI/ML
  elective and one Ethics in Computing elective before graduation.
- BS in Data Science (120 credits): Core sequence is DS101 Intro to Data Science -> DS201
  Statistics for Data Science -> DS250 Databases -> DS310 Machine Learning -> DS401 Capstone.
  Requires MATH210 Linear Algebra and MATH220 Probability as prerequisites for DS310.
- Minor in Artificial Intelligence (18 credits): CS201 Data Structures, CS330 Intro to AI,
  CS331 Machine Learning, and two AI electives.

PREREQUISITES
- CS201 Data Structures requires CS101 Intro to Programming (grade C or higher).
- CS301 Algorithms requires CS201 Data Structures.
- DS310 Machine Learning requires DS201 Statistics, MATH210 Linear Algebra, and MATH220
  Probability.
- CS401 / DS401 Capstone requires senior standing (90+ credits) and department approval.

REGISTRATION POLICIES
- Standard full-time load is 12–18 credits per semester. Loads above 18 credits require
  advisor sign-off.
- Add/Drop deadline is the end of week 2 of the semester. Withdrawals (W grade) are allowed
  through week 10.
- Waitlisted students are auto-enrolled if a seat opens before week 1 ends.

ACADEMIC STANDING
- Good standing requires a cumulative GPA of 2.0 or higher.
- A GPA below 2.0 for one semester results in Academic Warning; a second consecutive semester
  results in Academic Probation with a mandatory advising hold on registration.
- Students on probation are capped at 13 credits per semester until GPA recovers to 2.0+.

WHEN TO ESCALATE TO A HUMAN ADVISOR
Refer the student to the School of Computer & Data Sciences Advising Office
(advising@cascadestate.edu) for: financial aid or tuition questions, medical or personal
leave of absence, disciplinary/academic integrity cases, international/visa status, and any
situation not covered by this document.
